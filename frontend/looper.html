🅰 Pattern banks & chain

Four banks, each holding a drum pattern and a piano-roll pattern. The grids always show the current bank; edits stay in it.

A B C D (status bar)Switch bank. While the sequencer or roll is playing, the switch is queued (amber) and lands on the next bar line. A dot = the bank has notes
⧉ CopyClick ⧉, then a bank: the current bank is pasted into it (build a variation from a copy)
Chain boxBank order, one bar each — e.g. A A B A C (spaces optional, up to 16 bars). Turns red if it has a letter that isn't A–D
⛓ ChainOn: drums + roll follow the chain bar by bar (read-out shows bar 3/5). → Loop renders the whole chain into one loop, and ⬇ MIDI writes every bar. The chain has to match your loops' length (or divide it evenly) — you get a warning if it wouldn't line up
★ Save with Chain onA beat or melody favorite saved while Chain is on keeps all four banks' parts + the chain (shown with ⛓). Loading it restores them

🎛 MIDI Input

Play the Looper from a USB / Bluetooth MIDI keyboard or pad controller (Chrome / Edge). MIDI goes through the same path as the computer keys, so it's captured when a loop is recording.

🎹 ConnectAsk the browser for MIDI access (once — after that it reconnects automatically). Devices can be plugged / unplugged any time
Device menuListen to all inputs, or just one
KeysPlay the synth with velocity → loudness. Sustain pedal (CC64) holds notes. Arp, ADSR, filter, instrument and scale lock all apply. MIDI note 60 = middle C, whatever OCT is set to
🥁 MIDI → DrumsSend every note to the drum pads (channel 10 always does). GM map: 36 Kick · 35 → 808 · 38/40 Snare · 42/44 HiHat · 46 Open · 39 Clap · 41–50 Toms · anything else → Perc
🎥 Air Band (webcam)

Play the drums and keys with your hands in the air. The camera view is split into zones and every zone plays one sound. Hand tracking runs on your own GPU in the browser — no video is uploaded. Hits go through the same path as the keys and MIDI, so they are recorded into loops and follow scale lock, ADSR, filter, arp and the chosen instrument.

Before you startTurn on the browser's graphics acceleration or tracking crawls at 5 fps. Chrome: address bar → chrome://settings/system → "Use graphics acceleration when available" → Relaunch. With it on, the read-out says gpu and runs ~60 fps; the app will tell you if it's off
🎥 CameraTurns the webcam on (first time downloads the ≈10 MB hand tracker). Works in Chrome / Edge on localhost or https. Click again to stop
Drum zone (amber)A quick downward wrist flick = one hit, like a drumstick — the fingers/knuckles are what's measured, so no arm swing needed. Faster = louder. The hit lands in the zone the hand was in when the flick started. Zones flash when they fire
Note zone (green)Hold an open hand inside the zone and the note sustains; slide to another zone to change note; make a fist or leave to release. Two hands = two notes
Kit areaThe dashed box on the camera view is where the zones live — put it where a kit would be (default: the lower half). Drag it to move, drag a corner to resize, double-click to reset. Hands outside the box play nothing, so rest them above the kit between hits
LayoutDrum kit (8 pads, laid out like a kit: hi-hat left, snare and kick in front, tom right) · Keys (8 notes) · Band (drums left, chord tones right) · Chord tones · or your own Custom mix. Grid picks how many zones (rows × columns)
Zone boxesLaid out like the camera view — pick what each zone plays. Note names follow the Scale Lock key. Saved in this browser
HitStrike sensitivity. Left = needs a firm whack (no accidental hits), right = hair trigger. The bar on the right edge of the zone your hand is in is the strike meter: it fills as the hand moves down and fires when it reaches the white tick
Slow / laggy?The read-out shows the tracking rate and which tracker is running. Under ~12 fps on the GPU the app tries the CPU tracker automatically and the status explains why — almost always the graphics-acceleration setting above
No sound?Browsers only start audio after a click. The Camera click does it; if the read-out says 🔇, click anywhere on the page once
🪞 MirrorSelfie view: your left hand appears on the left. Off = camera view as-is
⛶ BigFull-window view for performing or recording a clip. Esc closes
TipsStand back so the whole hand is in frame · plain background and good light track best · hold the hand like a drumstick and flick from the wrist · a fist strikes cleaner than an open hand · rest hands above the kit area between phrases

🎶 Vocal Harmonizer

Add harmony voices to your microphone input — sing one note and hear up to two pitch-shifted harmony parts.

Harmony buttonToggle the harmonizer on/off (requires microphone to be enabled).
Voice 1Always active when harmonizer is on. Default: +4 semitones (major 3rd). Adjustable interval.
Voice 2Optional second harmony voice. Default: +7 semitones (perfect 5th). Toggle on/off independently.
IntervalsSet each voice to any interval from -12 to +12 semitones. Common choices: +3 (minor 3rd), +4 (major 3rd), +5 (perfect 4th), +7 (perfect 5th), +12 (octave).
How it worksRuns as an AudioWorklet after the autotune stage. Uses autocorrelation pitch detection and dual-grain pitch shifting for real-time harmony generation.
⏱ Transport & Master FX
BPM − / + · TapSet tempo by buttons or by tapping the beat
MetroToggle the metronome click
Count-inBeats of lead-in before recording starts
QuantizeSnap the first loop's length to the nearest bar
SwingPushes every off-beat 16th late. 0% = straight, 50% = light shuffle, 100% = full triplet feel (off-beats delayed ⅓ of a 16th). Shared by the drum sequencer and piano roll, and baked into → Loop renders and MIDI export
HumanizeRandom timing (up to ±15 ms) and velocity (±20%) per hit at 100%. Applies to playback and → Loop renders (not MIDI export)
Master FXGlobal Reverb, Delay (tempo-synced), and Volume
⬇ ExportRender all loops (one cycle, EQ + volume baked in) in the format picked in the top bar
Export format (top bar)WAV (16-bit) or MP3 320 kbps
Loudness target (top bar)Off, −14 LUFS (Spotify / YouTube), −16 LUFS (Apple), −9 LUFS (loud club master). The mix is gained to the target and a look-ahead limiter keeps true peak at or below −1 dBTP
LUFS read-outAfter each export: integrated loudness + true peak, e.g. −14.0 LUFS · −1.1 dBTP (hover for before/after + gain). Turns red if the peak is over −1 dBTP with loudness off

🎼 Song Builder

Turn your recorded loops into a full-length track. All loops run continuously on the grid; each section just chooses which ones are audible — so layers drop out and come back perfectly in time.

Auto-arrangeSeeds a build-up (loops enter one at a time) → groove → break → drop, repeated. Tweak the knobs and hit ↻ Generate.
Section blockClick the numbered chips to choose which loops play; − / + set the length in beats (4 beats = 1 bar); the VOL slider sets that section's level (breakdown softer, drop louder); rename it inline; ◀ ▶ reorder; ⧉ duplicate; × removes it.
Break blockA section with no loops on = silence for those beats. The next block brings the groove back in.
Master rowFade in/out, normalize to a target dBFS + soft limiter (stacked loops never clip), optionally bake in the master Reverb/Delay with a tail, and 16- or 24-bit output.
▶ PreviewAudition the whole arrangement (with all master processing) before saving.
⬇ Render Full TrackRenders the arrangement (per-loop EQ baked in) and downloads it as <name>-song.
format · loudnessFull track as WAV (16/24-bit) or MP3 320, optionally gained to −14 / −16 / −9 LUFS with true peak ≤ −1 dBTP (after normalize, limiter and fades). The LUFS · dBTP read-out appears next to the length. Stems stay WAV.
⬇ StemsEach loop as its own full-length WAV (arrangement applied, un-normalized so they sum cleanly) for mixing in a DAW.
⇪ Song / Stems → StudioWith a Studio window open, send the full track — or every stem as its own track — straight into the Studio to keep producing.
🎹 MIDI Export

Export your piano roll melody and drum pattern as a Standard MIDI File (.mid) for use in any DAW.

MIDI buttonDownloads the current piano roll + drum sequencer as a .mid file. The file contains a tempo track, a melody track (from the piano roll), and a drum track (channel 10, GM percussion mapping). With ⛓ Chain on, every bar of the chain is written in order.
FormatStandard MIDI File Format 1, 480 PPQN. Each sequencer step = one 16th note (120 ticks). Swing shifts off-beat 16ths; drum ratchets export as real repeated hits; probability steps export as always-on.
DrumsMapped to General MIDI percussion: Kick=36, Snare=38, Hi-Hat=42, Open HH=46, Clap=39, Tom=45, 808=35 (acoustic bass drum), Perc=56. Import reads this map back (plus 40 snare, 44 pedal hat, other toms; anything else → Perc).
MelodyNotes from the piano roll with correct MIDI note numbers, durations from held-note lengths.
