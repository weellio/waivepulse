🎚 Per-Track Knobs

Drag up/down to adjust · Scroll wheel for fine steps · Double-click to reset to default.

KnobRangeWhat it does
VOL0 – 150%Track volume. 100% = unity. Boosting above 100% can clip if multiple loud tracks are summed — use the limiter (LMT) to catch peaks.
PANL100 – R100Stereo position. C = center. Try panning drums slightly left, guitar slightly right to add width without changing the mix.
EQ7-bandOpens the per-track 7-band parametric EQ (high-pass · low shelf · three bells · high shelf · low-pass) with a live spectrum and draggable curve. This is the only per-track EQ — the old SUB/BASS/MID/TREB knobs were retired in favour of it. AI music consistently lacks sub-bass (~60 Hz) and air (~10 kHz); shape those here.
REV0 – 100%Reverb send. Adds acoustic space — room, hall, depth. All tracks share one convolution reverb. A little goes a long way; too much washes out the mix.
DLY0 – 100%Delay send. Echo effect (~350ms, ~38% feedback). All tracks share one delay line. Works well on vocals and guitar for rhythmic depth.
OFS0 – 50 msTiming offset — delays this track by up to 50ms. Classic use: duplicate a stem (DUP button), set OFS to 10–20ms on the copy. This is called ADT (automatic double tracking) and makes thin AI audio sound fuller, like it was recorded twice.
🔴 Mute Automation

Draw silent sections on individual tracks — useful for acappella refrains, drum solos, instrumental breakdowns, or call-and-response sections.

Shift + drag on waveformPaint a red mute region. Hold shift, click, and drag left or right. The track goes silent only during those seconds.
Click inside a red regionDelete that mute region
Delete / BackspaceClear ALL mute regions on the selected track

Mute regions are active during live playback and are baked into the Export Mix WAV file.

✂ Cut (ripple delete)

Mute leaves a silent gap; Cut removes a section from every track at once and closes the gap, so the whole song gets shorter — ideal for trimming dead air, a long intro, or a section you don't want.

1. SelectDrag across the ruler (or set [ and ]) to highlight the region
2. ✂ CutRemoves that region from all tracks and ripples everything after it left. Confirms first and shows the new length.
↩ UncutUndo the last cut (multiple levels)

Imported clips after the cut slide left to stay in time; the shortened song is what gets exported.

✨ Mastering Tools

AI-generated music often sounds thinner and flatter than professionally recorded music. These tools help close that gap.

MASTER buttonSits at the right of the Mix bar. One-click mastering preset: duplicates drums at 12ms offset (ADT thickening), applies conservative per-stem EQ (sub and bass boost on drums/bass, treble air on vocals/piano, a slight mid scoop on vocals to compensate), adds reverb presence to instruments, enables the harmonic exciter at a low wet level, boosts the master sub and air EQ modestly, and enables the clipper to catch peaks. Use this as a starting point, then tweak individual knobs — and RST ALL (right next to it) fully undoes it.
EXC (Exciter)Harmonic exciter on the master bus. High-passes the signal at 3kHz, applies soft saturation to only the high frequencies, then mixes it back at 18% wet. Adds overtone shimmer and "air" that AI audio typically lacks — makes it sound more alive and present without affecting the low end.
LMT (Limiter)Master bus limiter/compressor. Tames loud peaks and adds a sense of loudness glue. Settings: −18dB threshold, 4:1 ratio, fast attack. Toggle on for a more radio-ready sound. Mutually exclusive with CLP.
CLP (Clipper)Master bus soft clipper. Catches transient peaks at a −0.5 dBFS ceiling using a tanh-bent waveshaper (4× oversampled) while leaving everything below the knee untouched. Preserves drum/transient punch where a limiter would duck them — often the better choice for AI-generated music that needs loudness without losing impact. Mutually exclusive with LMT.
VOL sliderMaster output volume — scales the entire mix from 0% (silence) to 150% (boost). Affects both live playback and Export Mix. Use it to bring down a mix that's too hot before exporting, or to match levels when A/B comparing. Default is 100%.
RST ALL buttonNext to MASTER in the Mix bar. Resets the whole mix back to the original Full Mix in one click — every track's knobs (VOL 100%, PAN center, REV/DLY/OFS 0) and per-track EQ flat, plus the entire master chain: limiter/clipper/exciter/gate/crusher/folder/plate off, master sub/air EQ flat, master volume 100%, and un-mutes everything (Full Mix). This fully undoes MASTER and any genre/saved preset. Your tracks are kept — use a track's ✕ to remove an extra (e.g. a duplicate). Individual tracks also have their own RST button, and R resets the selected track.
DUP buttonDuplicate any stem as its own independent track. Set OFS to 10–20ms on the copy for ADT doubling. Try panning the two copies slightly apart (e.g. L20 and R20) for a wide stereo image.
EQ buttonOpens the per-track 7-band parametric EQ — live spectrum analyzer + a draggable curve (high-pass · low shelf · three bells · high shelf · low-pass), each with freq/gain/Q. This is the only per-track EQ (the old SUB/BASS/MID/TREB knobs were retired in favour of it). Esc to close.
FADE in / outMaster fade sliders (0–15 s) in the FX bar. Smoothly ramps the whole mix up at the start and down at the end — perfect for cleaning up a hard AI stop. The fade-out is measured back from the current song end, so it automatically follows a Cut. Catches reverb/exciter/plate tails too, and is baked into Export Mix.

Tip: All mastering settings — master EQ, exciter, limiter, clipper, master volume, fades, per-track EQ — are fully baked into Export Mix. What you hear is what you get.

🎛 Mix Presets & Genre Templates

Save and recall full mixer configurations — all per-track knobs, EQ, effects, and master chain settings in one click.

Genre templatesFour built-in starting points: Radio Pop, Lo-Fi Hip Hop, Rock, EDM. Each sets per-stem volumes, reverb, master EQ, exciter, and other chain settings to suit the genre.
SAVESnapshot the current full mixer state as a named preset. Saved to browser localStorage — persists across sessions.
Click a saved presetRestores every knob, EQ band, effect toggle, and master chain parameter to the saved values.
× on a presetDelete that saved preset.

Presets gracefully skip missing stems — a preset saved from a 6-stem song works on one with fewer.

🔗 Side-Chain Ducking

Automatically duck one track when another plays — classic technique for making bass duck on kick hits.

SC toggle in FX barEnable/disable sidechain ducking.
Key dropdownThe track that triggers ducking (default: drums).
Target dropdownThe track that gets ducked (default: bass).
Threshold sliderLevel the key signal must exceed to trigger ducking (-60 to 0 dB).
Ratio sliderHow much to duck (1:1 = no ducking, 20:1 = heavy ducking).
📥 Importing Tracks

Bring in any audio file as a full mixer track — samples, loops, scratch vocals, backing tracks.

＋ Track buttonOpen file picker. Accepts MP3, WAV, FLAC, OGG, M4A, AAC.
Drag & dropDrop one or more audio files anywhere onto the Studio window. A drop target appears.
Drag the clip blockClick and drag the gold waveform block left or right on the timeline to set where in the song it plays. The cursor turns to a grab hand when hovering over the block. The clip is clamped so it can't go past the song boundaries. Works at any zoom level.
⟳ buttonLoop this track. A short sample will repeat continuously for the full song duration. Loop state is baked into Export Mix. (Looping disables clip positioning — the clip plays from time 0.)
× buttonRemove the imported track permanently
BPM auto-detectionWhen you drag-drop a file, its BPM is auto-detected in the background and displayed on the track strip.
Time-stretch controlsEnter the current BPM and a target BPM, then click STRETCH to tempo-match the imported track without changing pitch.
Auto-stretch promptWhen importing a stem from the Stem Library whose BPM differs from the current song, you're automatically prompted to time-stretch it to match.
🎵 Chord Detection

Automatic chord analysis displayed above the timeline.

CHORDS buttonToggle the chord overlay on/off. When on, detected chords (e.g. C major, Am, G) appear as yellow labels between the ruler and the first track.
How it worksThe server analyzes the song using librosa chroma analysis and template matching (24 chord templates). Results are cached after the first request.
ZoomChord labels scale with the zoom level so they stay aligned with the timeline.
🔄 Stem Swap

Import individual stems from other songs you've separated — mix vocals from one song with the backing track of another.

Stem Swap buttonOpens the Stem Library modal showing all stems from other separated songs.
Song cardsEach song shows its title, BPM, and key. Stems are colored buttons (green = vocals, orange = drums, etc.).
Click a stemImports it as a new track with full mixer controls.
Auto-stretch promptIf the imported stem's BPM differs from the current song, you're prompted to time-stretch it to match (pitch preserved).
EscClose the Stem Library modal.
⏩ Time-Stretch

Change the tempo of an imported track without affecting pitch — essential for matching vocals from one song to the backing track of another.

BPM labelImported tracks show their detected BPM (auto-detected in the background when you drag-drop a file).
Target BPMEnter the desired tempo (pre-filled with the current song's BPM).
STRETCH buttonSends the audio to the server for phase-vocoder time-stretching. The stretched audio replaces the track buffer — pitch is preserved, only tempo changes.
Stretch range0.25× to 4.0× (quarter speed to quadruple speed).
Re-stretchingThe original buffer is kept, so re-stretching doesn't compound quality loss.
🔁 Variation & Splice

Generate variations of a song and splice sections into the current mix.

Variation buttonOpens the Generate page pre-filled with the same lyrics and tags but slightly higher temperature (+0.1) for a fresh take on the same song.
Splice buttonSelect a region on the ruler, click Splice, then choose an audio file. The selected region is replaced with the new audio, crossfaded at the boundaries. Works on all stems simultaneously.
💾 Export
Audio info barDisplays sample rate, channel count, and — when available — detected BPM and musical key (e.g. "44.1 kHz · stereo · 120 BPM · C major").
⬇ Export MixRenders the full mix to a lossless 16-bit WAV file. All knobs, EQ, reverb, delay, mute regions, looped imports, and the master chain are baked in. This is your final output — use it for sharing, uploading, or loading into a DAW.
⬇ StemsDownload the six raw separated stems as a ZIP. Use them in any DAW (Pro Tools, Logic, Ableton, etc.) for full professional editing.
